Security boundary:
- Seeded credentials and the included signed-token authentication are for local MVP demonstrations only.
- Rotate AUTH_SECRET_KEY, change seeded credentials, disable demo-user seeding and arrange a security review before shared/public deployment.
- The district administrator can create and deactivate department accounts. Public self-registration is disabled.

Data/clinical limitation:
- The included monitoring zones and GeoJSON are illustrative, not official administrative boundaries.
- Health-service and workforce records are synthetic and aggregated.
- OpenAQ requires an API key. Open-Meteo weather refresh needs network access. CPCB AQI may be imported from a sourced official CSV/export.
- Risk coefficients and alert thresholds are prototype parameters, not clinically validated thresholds.
