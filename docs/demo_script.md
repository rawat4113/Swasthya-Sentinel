# 5–7 minute hackathon demonstration

1. **Sign in (30 sec):** Open the browser app and sign in as the District Administrator. Show that department accounts have different navigation and input permissions.
2. **Department input (75 sec):** Sign out and sign in as the Health Department Officer. Enter an aggregated clinic/symptom observation through the browser form. Show the confirmation and the user's submission history. Sign in as the Environmental Officer to show the environmental-only form, if time permits.
3. **Bulk upload (45 sec):** Return as admin or a matching department user. Upload a sample CSV, review the backend validation result and commit valid rows.
4. **Command Center (45 sec):** Show the illustrative zones, heat and respiratory risk scores, confidence and open alerts.
5. **AI explanation (60 sec):** Inspect the signal contributions, historical baseline and confidence. Explain that weights and severity bands are demonstration settings.
6. **Simulation (60 sec):** As administrator, select a zone, inject a heatwave or pollution event and show the simulation-only result and alert.
7. **Prevention workflow (45 sec):** Acknowledge a warning, assign the responsible role and add an intervention note.
8. **Validation and privacy (45 sec):** Show synthetic known-event backtest metrics, small-cell suppression and role-scoped submission history. State clearly that simulated evaluation is not clinical validation.

Suggested closing: “Swasthya Sentinel connects environmental, health-service and workplace signals; explains why a zone may need attention; and translates the warning into a traceable preventive workflow.”
